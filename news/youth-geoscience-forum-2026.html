Outstanding Student Presentation Award at the 11th Youth Geoscience Forum

English / 中文

Yuanrui Chen, a Ph.D. candidate in Jonathon S. Wright’s group at the Department of Earth System Science, Tsinghua University, received an Outstanding Student Presentation Award at the 11th Youth Geoscience Forum. The forum was held in Guangzhou, China, on 10–13 April 2026, bringing together more than 6,800 participants from across the Earth sciences.

His presentation focused on interactions between the South Pacific Convergence Zone (SPCZ) and convectively coupled equatorial waves. Using daily outgoing longwave radiation as a proxy for convection, the study identifies equatorial Rossby waves as the largest contributor among the equatorial wave bands examined to SPCZ convective variability. As these waves encounter the SPCZ, their structures sharpen and their westward propagation slows.

Diagnostics reveal a wave–evaporation–convection feedback, reinforced by the SPCZ background state, that shapes wave growth and propagation. The work was conducted with Chang Xu, Steven Sherwood, and Jonathon S. Wright and is now published in the Journal of Climate.

陈元瑞获第十一届青年地学论坛优秀学生报告奖

清华大学地球系统科学系 Jonathon S. Wright（乔纳森）课题组博士生陈元瑞在第十一届青年地学论坛上获得优秀学生报告奖。本届论坛于2026年4月10日至13日在广州举行，吸引了来自地学领域的6,800余名代表参会。

报告围绕南太平洋辐合带（SPCZ）与对流耦合赤道波的相互作用展开。研究以逐日向外长波辐射表征对流活动，发现赤道罗斯贝波在所考察的赤道波频带中，对 SPCZ 对流变率的贡献最大。这些波动与 SPCZ 相遇后，波动结构变得更为尖锐，向西传播的速度减慢。

进一步的动力诊断揭示了波动—蒸发—对流反馈对波动增长和传播的作用，以及 SPCZ 背景态对这一反馈的增强。该工作由陈元瑞与 Chang Xu、Steven Sherwood 和 Jonathon S. Wright 合作完成，现已发表于 Journal of Climate。
