Outstanding Student Presentation Award at the 11th Youth Geoscience Forum

English / 中文

Yuanrui Chen, a Ph.D. candidate in Associate Professor Jonathon Wright’s group at the Department of Earth System Science, Tsinghua University, received an Outstanding Student Presentation Award at the 11th Youth Geoscience Forum in Guangzhou, China.

Chen’s presentation focused on interactions between the South Pacific Convergence Zone (SPCZ) and convectively coupled equatorial waves. The study quantified the important contribution of these waves to SPCZ convective variability and clarified how a vorticity–evaporation–convection feedback modulates wave structure and propagation during interactions between equatorial Rossby waves and the SPCZ. These findings provide a new dynamical framework for understanding tropical wave–convection coupling in the SPCZ and its representation in models. The work was completed during Chen’s visit to UNSW Sydney, Australia, in collaboration with Chang Xu and Steven Sherwood at its Climate Change Research Centre (CCRC).

The forum was held in Guangzhou on 10–13 April 2026. Dedicated to promoting exchange and innovation among early-career Earth scientists, it brought together more than 6,800 participants from across the Earth sciences.

陈元瑞获第十一届青年地学论坛优秀学生报告奖

在中国广州举办的第十一届青年地学论坛上，清华大学地学系 Jonathon Wright（乔纳森）副教授课题组博士生陈元瑞荣获优秀学生报告奖。

陈元瑞的报告围绕南太平洋辐合带（SPCZ）与对流耦合赤道波的相互作用展开。研究定量揭示了对流耦合赤道波对 SPCZ 对流变率的重要贡献，并阐明了赤道罗斯贝波与 SPCZ 相互作用过程中“涡度—蒸发—对流”反馈对波动结构和传播特征的调制作用，为理解热带波动与 SPCZ 对流耦合及其模式表述提供了新的动力学框架。该工作由陈元瑞在澳大利亚新南威尔士大学（UNSW）访问期间完成，合作研究人员包括该校气候变化研究中心（CCRC）的 Chang Xu 和 Steven Sherwood。

本届论坛于2026年4月10日至13日在广州举行，以促进青年地学研究人员“互动交流、创新发展”为宗旨，吸引了来自地学领域的6,800余名代表参会。
